Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the club?

Simply click the "Sign Up" button and fill out the registration form. Once approved, you'll receive an email with further instructions.

Do I need prior experience?

Not at all! We welcome members of all skill levels, from complete beginners to advanced roboticists. We have workshops tailored for everyone.

What equipment do I need?

We provide all necessary equipment and tools in our lab. However, having your own laptop is recommended for programming workshops.

Is there a membership fee?

We have a minimal annual fee of $50 to cover equipment maintenance and event costs. Financial assistance is available for those who need it.
